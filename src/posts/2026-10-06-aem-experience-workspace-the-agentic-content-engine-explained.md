What if you could build a complete, on-brand landing page from **one prompt**, inside AEM? That's one of the demos in our new Adobe Developers Live session, where my colleague Martin Buergi and I introduce **AEM Experience Workspace**. If you followed my posts on [DA MCP](/da-mcp/) or our [Summit 2026 lab](/da-vide-coding-summit-lab/), this is where that work is heading.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b8IeoY4SKBI

## What is Experience Workspace?

It's the AI-first evolution of AEM: one canvas where authors, developers and AI agents work on the same content. The visual editing is based on Universal Editor principles, so a lot of it will feel familiar.

## What you'll see in the video

- **Three views of one page:** visual, raw content, and a split view that keeps both in sync.
- **Live collaboration:** you can see when a colleague is editing the page. You can also see when an AI agent is.
- **An AI side panel that knows what you've selected** and works with your brand guidelines and SEO requirements.
- **Skills and MCP:** Martin pulls products from Adobe Commerce and builds a landing page from a single prompt. My favourite part.

## Try it yourself

In the second half I show how to get started. It's pretty simple:

1. **Just for you:** flip the *Experience Workspace* toggle in the AEM top navigation. You can switch back anytime.
2. **For a whole site or org:** run the setup wizard, or set `ew_enabled: true` in your site configuration sheet.
3. **Add skills:** connect pre-built or your own skill repositories, for example from GitHub.
